Before you begin

This is a 60-item diagnostic test on the use, protocols and assumptions of parametric and non-parametric procedures. Read each item, choose the best answer, and select Next; the following item appears only once you have answered. No score is shown on screen — your responses go to your instructor, who returns a profile of your strong and weak areas.
